YK Pao School to Launch Yau Tseng Class from 2026-27 Academic Year

Date: April 1, 2026
Author: YK Pao School
Views: 15

With authorization from Professor Shing-Tung Yau, internationally renowned mathematician and Director of the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center at Tsinghua University, YK Pao School will officially launch the Yau Tseng Class (hereinafter referred to as the “Yau Class”) in the 2026-27 academic year. This marks an important step for our school in the early cultivation of top-tier innovative talents and makes YK Pao School the fifth school in Shanghai authorized to offer the Yau Class.


The Yau Tseng Class is an early talent development program initiated and personally guided by Professor Yau. It aims to identify secondary school students with exceptional potential in mathematics and physics, and through an integrated curriculum spanning middle and high school—coupled with collaboration between secondary schools and universities—nurture future leaders in fundamental sciences. Currently, more than 50 secondary schools across 19 provinces and municipalities in China have been authorized to offer the Yau Class. In Shanghai, four schools previously received authorization: No. 2 High School of East China Normal University, Shanghai High School, Fudan University Affiliated High School, and High School Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University. YK Pao School is now the fifth.


The Yau Tseng Class emphasizes early cultivation, allowing students as young as 13 or 14 who are passionate about mathematics to receive guidance from top-tier faculty and develop a solid foundation in mathematical tools and knowledge. The curriculum focuses on stimulating students’ interest and independent thinking, avoiding the pitfalls of rote memorization and mechanical problem‑solving. In 2025, Professor Yau personally led the development of the curriculum for the Yau Class, with contributions from leading scientists such as Academician Tian Ye of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, creating a first‑class mathematics and science curriculum that bridges secondary and university education.


The introduction of the Yau Tseng Class represents a significant milestone in YK Pao School’s mission to “develop the whole person, inherit Chinese culture, and expand global perspectives.” The school will leverage the high‑quality educational resources of Tsinghua University’s Qiuzhen College, combined with Pao School’s deep expertise in international education, to provide a broader platform for outstanding students aspiring to pursue fundamental research. We believe this initiative will contribute to the early identification and cultivation of top‑notch innovative talents in Shanghai and across China.


Looking ahead, YK Pao School will further refine the admissions and training framework for the Yau Tseng Class, strengthen collaborative mechanisms with leading universities, and enable more mathematically gifted students to thrive in a nurturing environment. The school will continue to drive innovation in education, exploring new pathways for the development of China’s future leaders in fundamental disciplines.
